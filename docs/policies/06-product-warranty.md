# Product Warranty Overview

## Manufacturer warranty
Most electronics include a manufacturer warranty of 12 months from purchase. Customers should contact the brand first unless NovaMart Care+ was purchased.

## NovaMart Care+
Optional protection plan sold at checkout:
- Covers accidental damage after a $29 service fee.
- Claims start in ShelfMate under Care+ → New Claim.
- Plan is non-refundable after 30 days.

## Defective within 7 days
Treat as a standard return/exchange with no restocking fee when the item fails under normal use.
