# Shipping Rates & Promises

## Standard shipping
- Orders under $35: flat $5.99.
- Orders $35+: free standard shipping (3–5 business days).

## Express
- 2-day: $12.99.
- Next-day (order before 12:00 local warehouse time): $19.99.

## Store associates
Do not promise same-day delivery unless the order tool shows a same-day eligible flag. Escalate edge cases to Ops.
