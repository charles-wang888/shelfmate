# Price Match Policy

NovaMart matches the current advertised price of identical items from approved local competitors and novamart.com.

## Eligible
- Same SKU / model number, new condition.
- Competitor price must be publicly advertised and in stock.
- Request must be made at purchase time or within **7 days** with receipt.

## Not eligible
- Marketplace third-party sellers, auction sites, membership-club pricing, clearance endings in *.97, and bundle deals.

## Process
1. Capture competitor proof (screenshot or flyer).
2. Apply price-match adjustment in POS using reason code PMATCH.
3. For online competitor matches above $50 difference, escalate to Ops for verification.
