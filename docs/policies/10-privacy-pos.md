# Customer Privacy at the POS

## Do
- Verify identity with government ID for high-value pickups and returns over $200.
- Mask payment details on printed receipts when reprinting.

## Do not
- Share customer phone numbers, emails, or order history with other customers.
- Photograph IDs or store them in personal devices.
- Discuss customer orders on the sales floor loudspeaker.

Violations must be escalated to Ops and Loss Prevention the same day.
