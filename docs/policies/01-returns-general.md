# NovaMart General Returns Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-01

## Summary
Customers may return most unused items within **30 days** of purchase with a receipt or order confirmation.

## Conditions
- Item must be unused, in original packaging when practical, and include all accessories.
- Gift receipts are accepted; refunds without proof of purchase are issued as store credit at the current selling price.
- Final-sale items (clearance stickers, opened software, personal-care opened packages) are not returnable.

## Refund method
- Card purchases: refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
- Cash / store credit: refunded as store credit unless a manager approves cash.

## In-store process
1. Verify receipt or look up the order in ShelfMate / POS.
2. Inspect condition and attach a return reason code.
3. Issue refund or exchange and place the item in the returns tote.
