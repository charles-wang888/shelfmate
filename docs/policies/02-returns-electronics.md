# Electronics Returns & Exchanges

## Return window
- Unopened electronics: **30 days**.
- Opened electronics (TVs, laptops, headphones, smart home): **15 days**.
- Carrier-locked phones activated on a plan: **14 days**, subject to carrier restocking rules.

## Special rules
- Must include all cables, remotes, and serial-matched box labels.
- Devices with signs of liquid damage or missing activation locks (Find My / FRP) cannot be accepted.
- Large appliances and TVs over 40" require a manager override for doorstep pickup scheduling.

## Restocking fee
A 15% restocking fee may apply to opened electronics unless defective within the first 7 days.
