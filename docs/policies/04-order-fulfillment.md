# Order Fulfillment & Pickup

## Status meanings
- `processing`: payment captured; warehouse has not shipped.
- `out_for_delivery`: carrier has the parcel; ETA is shown in the order tool.
- `delivered`: customer confirmed or carrier scan complete.
- `cancelled`: order stopped before shipment; refunds post in 3–5 business days.

## BOPIS (Buy Online Pickup In Store)
- Orders are held at Customer Service for **5 days** after ready notification.
- ID matching the order name is required for pickup.
- After 5 days, unclaimed orders are cancelled and restocked.

## Damaged on arrival
Photograph damage, keep packaging, and create a claims ticket for Ops within 48 hours of delivery.
